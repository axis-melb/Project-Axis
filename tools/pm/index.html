§01 WHY THIS EXISTS / 初衷
THE PROBLEM / 痛点

Site work often involves patchy reception and sensitive project data. General project apps are either too complex or require constant internet access to sync.

建筑工地的信号往往不稳定且涉及项目敏感信息。市面上的软件要么功能冗余，要么必须实时联网才能同步。

  • Complex enterprise software is overkill for individual consultants
  • Poor mobile experience in traditional spreadsheets
  • Risk of data exposure on centralized project platforms
THE SOLUTION / 这里提供的逻辑
  • Instant access via mobile browser, even in low-signal zones
  • Focus on the essentials: Category, Progress, Value, and Notes
  • Local storage ensures total privacy of your commercial data
  • Exportable project histories for backup or reporting

§05 DATA SAFETY / 数据安全

Your client data is stored in your browser's localStorage. To switch devices or back up, use the built-in export function.
您的客户数据存储在浏览器本地。如需更换设备或备份，请使用内置导出功能。

  • → Regularly hit Export (JSON) to save a backup to your local device. (I save mine to my iCloud.)
    定期点击 导出 (JSON) 以保存备份至本地（建议存入 iCloud 或手机文件夹）。
  • → On a new device (such as an iPad), open the tool and select Import to restore your data.
    在新设备（如 iPad）上打开此页面，选择 导入 即可恢复数据。
  • → Completely private. No data is shared with third parties.
    完全私密。数据绝不与任何第三方共享。
