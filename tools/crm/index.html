§01 WHY THIS EXISTS / 为什么做这个
THE PROBLEM / 痛点

I'm tired of complex CRMs that compromise privacy. I need a lightweight way to track my local clients without cloud overhead.

我发现管理几十个客户并不需要庞大的 CRM 系统，而 Excel 又缺乏自动到期提醒。更重要的是，我不希望客户隐私资料被上传到第三方的云端数据库。

  • Traditional CRMs are expensive and require server-side databases / 传统 CRM 昂贵且涉及服务器维护
  • Excel needs manually tracking of policy anniversaries / Excel 难以自动跟踪保单周年期
  • Data privacy risks when using third-party SaaS platforms / 第三方 SaaS 平台存在泄露客户资料的风险
THE SOLUTION / 这里提供的逻辑
  • An offline-first tool. Pure HTML link. Privacy by default / 纯 HTML 链接，本地优先，隐私至上
  • 30-day "Red Alert" for policy anniversaries. Contact clients on time / 到期前30天自动飘红，准时联系客户
  • Self-filled commission rates for instant financial overview / 自填佣金比例，快速预览财务收益
  • Full JSON export. You own your data forever / 完整的 JSON 数据导出，永不丢失控制权
§02 WHAT IT DOES / 功能
F.001
ANNIVERSARY ALERT

When a policy anniversary is within 30 days, the client entry turns red. No more missing premium deadlines. / 保单周年日前30天自动飘红提醒，提醒客户准时续保。

F.002
COMMISSION CALCULATOR

Input policy start date, duration, and annual premium. Customize your commission rate to see your earnings instantly. / 记录开始时间、年期和保费，自主填写佣金率以计算收益。

F.003
CLIENT NOTES

Keep ad-hoc notes for each client — follow-up history, specific needs, or family details. Everything stays local. / 为每个客户记录详细备注，支持个性化跟踪与快速查阅。

§05 YOUR DATA / 数据安全与备份

Your client data is stored in your browser's localStorage. To switch devices or back up, use the built-in export function.

  • → Regularly hit Export (JSON) to save a backup to your local device.
  • → On a new device, open the tool and select Import to restore.
  • → Completely private. No data is shared with third parties.
