§01 WHY THIS EXISTS
THE PROBLEM

I'm tired of complex CRMs that compromise privacy. I need a lightweight way to track clients without cloud overhead, plus automatic reminders for policy anniversaries.

  • Traditional CRMs are expensive and require server-side infrastructure
  • Spreadsheets require manual tracking of policy anniversary dates
  • Third-party SaaS platforms carry real data privacy risks for client information
THE SOLUTION
  • Offline-first. Pure HTML. No server, no account, no vendor lock-in
  • 30-day red alert before policy anniversaries — never miss a premium deadline
  • Self-filled commission rates for an instant financial overview
  • Full JSON export — you own your data, permanently
§02 WHAT IT DOES
F.001
ANNIVERSARY ALERT

When a policy anniversary falls within 30 days, the client entry turns red. No more missed premium deadlines or lapsed policies.

F.002
COMMISSION CALCULATOR

Input policy start date, duration, and annual premium. Set your own commission rate to see projected earnings at a glance.

F.003
CLIENT NOTES

Store follow-up history, specific needs, or family context for each client. Everything lives locally — never leaves your device.

§05 YOUR DATA

Your client data is stored in your browser's localStorage. To back up or move to a new device, use the built-in export function.

  • → Regularly hit Export (JSON) to save a backup to your local device or cloud storage (iCloud, Google Drive).
  • → On a new device, open the tool and select Import to restore your full client list.
  • → Completely private. No data is ever shared with third parties.
